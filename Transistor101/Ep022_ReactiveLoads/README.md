In this episode, we walk through nearly all the steps of electrical and thermal design for a class-AB power amp, taking into account the reactive load that it must drive. We cover measuring the impedance of the speaker, calculating power requirements, selecting the output transistor pair and heatsink, sizing the ballast resistors, and designing the Sziklai driver stage and bias servo.

I don't quite get as far as building anything, because I also want to adapt the input stage to higher power. That comes next!
